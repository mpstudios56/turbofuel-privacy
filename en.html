Turbofuel privacy policy

Effective from 21 November 2026

Turbofuel is an Android app that keeps track of the maintenance of motorbikes, scooters, cars and vans: mileage, services, deadlines, expenses and vehicle papers. Turbofuel is designed so that your data stays on your phone unless you choose otherwise.

1. Who we are

Turbofuel is developed by Marcello Pandolfo (MP Studios).
For any question you can write to marcellopandolfo@gmail.com.

2. What data the app handles

  • Vehicle data: make, model, year, number plate, VIN, technical data, mileage, purchase and insurance details, notes.
  • Maintenance and expenses: services, components, refuelling, costs, deadlines (road tax, insurance, roadworthiness test and others), pre-ride checks.
  • Vehicle papers: photos or PDFs of the registration document, ownership certificate, insurance and historic vehicle (ASI) registration, with the details read from them (number, issuer, holder, dates).
  • Workshops: name, address, phone, email and, if you choose, their location.
  • Garages: name and place, to organise your vehicles.

Entering this data is always voluntary.

3. Where it is kept

On your device, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and in the backups you create.

The developer does not receive, collect or store your data. Turbofuel has no servers of its own, requires no account, and contains no adverts, analytics or tracking tools.

4. When data leaves the device

Only when you explicitly choose so:

  • Backup to Google Drive (optional). If you switch it on, you sign in with your Google account and a copy of your data, papers included, is saved to your Google Drive, in a folder dedicated to Turbofuel. The app can only access the files it creates itself (the «drive.file» permission), not the rest of your Drive. The account's email address is only used to show you which account is connected. The transfer is encrypted (HTTPS). You can disconnect the account at any time and delete the copies from your Drive. Google's privacy policy also applies.
  • Sharing and export (PDF service book, CSV files, backups, papers). The app opens the Android share menu: you choose the app and the recipient.
  • Ordering TurboScan tags. If you order a tag, Turbofuel prepares an email to the developer with the vehicle details (type, make, model, year, plate), the tag design and the shipping details you enter (name, address, phone, email). The email is sent from your own mail app, only when you press send. This data is used solely to prepare and ship the order and is not passed to third parties, except what the courier needs for delivery. The shipping details stay saved on the phone for later orders.
  • Maps and places. When you look up a workshop's position or a garage's place, the text you type (address or town) is passed to the phone's location service, which may ask Google to find it. Opening a workshop on the map opens the Maps app with that address.

5. Camera and text recognition

The camera is used only when you choose to read the mileage from the dashboard or to photograph a document. Numbers and text are recognised on the phone itself; images are not sent over the Internet. Mileage images are not saved; photos and PDFs of papers stay in the app's private storage.

6. Location

Your location is read once, and only when you press «La mia posizione» (My location) on a workshop's card, to save its coordinates. Turbofuel does not track your movements and does not use location in the background.

7. NFC tags

The NFC permission is used to read and write TurboScan tags. The tag only holds a link telling Turbofuel which vehicle to open and which action to take, with no personal data.

8. Calendar and notifications

If you grant the calendar permission and switch the feature on, Turbofuel adds and updates your vehicles' deadlines, with reminders, in the phone's calendar, and only recognises the events it created itself. Turbofuel does not copy, store or send any other event. Notifications (upcoming deadlines, components due, mileage reminders) are generated on the phone.

9. Purchases

Any in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: Turbofuel has no access to payment details.

10. How long

Data stays on the device until you delete it. Deleting an item in the app, or uninstalling Turbofuel, removes the data held in the app. Backups saved on the phone, on Google Drive or shared elsewhere remain until you delete them. Tag order emails are kept by the developer for as long as needed to handle the order and for legal obligations.

11. Your rights and data deletion

You can view, change, export and delete your data at any time from within the app. For data sent with a tag order you can request access, correction or deletion by writing to the address above.

How to request deletion. Write to marcellopandolfo@gmail.com with the subject «Turbofuel – data deletion», giving the email or name used in the order. Within 30 days the order emails and shipping details are deleted, except data the law requires us to keep (for example for accounting), kept only for the time required. Data saved in the app is deleted from the app itself or by uninstalling it; copies on Google Drive are deleted from your Drive.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

12. Children

Turbofuel is intended for adults.

13. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page, together with the date they take effect.
